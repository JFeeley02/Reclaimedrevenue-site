How it works

No new software. Your team keeps doing what it does best.

  1. You send the leads that haven't booked

    A simple list or an export from whatever you already use.

  2. We agree on your criteria

    Over a quick call or by email, we agree on who to book, who not to book, and what to confirm first. You approve the criteria before we start.

  3. We call, qualify, and book

    Real people follow up by phone and book the ones who fit your criteria onto your calendar.

  4. You get every outcome, and what we learned

    Every lead gets an outcome in your monthly report, along with what we heard on the calls: why people didn't book, when they were easiest to reach, and anything worth changing on your end. If you'd like, we'll go over it together on a 15-minute call.

What we don't do

Your customers are your reputation. We treat every call that way.

  • No AI. No call center. Nobody overseas. We're a homegrown, independently owned business based in the greater Houston area. We listen, keep notes, and respect your customers' boundaries without ever losing sight of the booking. If someone says "call me after the 15th," we call on the 16th.
  • We are not a lead generation company. We work your leads. Odds are you don't need another pile. You need someone to help work the one you have.
  • No quoting your prices or making promises on your behalf.

  • No long contracts. Month-to-month, or start with a small trial batch.

Questions business owners frequently ask

Do you generate new leads?

No. We don't run ads or sell lead lists. We work the leads your business already brought in, from any source: calls, web forms, texts, quotes, referrals. If they reached out and never made it onto your calendar, or fell off it, we can work them.

Who makes the calls?

Real people based in the greater Houston area. Not AI, not an auto-dialer, and not a call center racing through a list. We introduce ourselves as calling on your behalf, then follow your criteria for who to book.

Do I need to set up any software?

No. A simple list or an export from whatever you use now is enough to get started.

What do you need from me?

Your leads, your criteria for who to book (over a quick call or by email), and your do-not-contact list if you have one. We handle the booking however you prefer: straight onto your calendar, through your scheduling tool, or handed off to someone on your team.

What does the free list assessment involve?

A 10-minute phone call, or a few quick questions by email. You pull up wherever your leads live, answer a few questions, and get an honest read on whether there's anything worth chasing. You never send us customer info.

How many times will you call each lead?

Up to six tries over about two weeks, at different times of day and only during normal hours. Anyone who asks us to stop is taken off right away.

What do I get each month?

A report with every appointment we booked and an outcome for every lead you sent, plus what we learned from talking to your customers: the most common reasons people didn't book, what times reached them, and anything worth changing on your end, like mentioning financing on your estimates. If you'd like, we'll walk through it together on a 15-minute call. We only report what the calls actually showed, and if it's too early to tell, we'll say so.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many leads come to us each month. You pay a flat monthly fee based on that volume, plus a flat fee for each appointment we book that meets your criteria. Nothing per call or per hour, and it's month to month. The appointment fee is due whether or not you win the job, and the size of the job doesn't affect it. You'll get exact numbers in writing after the free assessment, before any work starts.
