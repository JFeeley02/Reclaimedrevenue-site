Where leads slip through

You already paid to get these people to reach out. Most businesses have more of them sitting untouched than they'd guess.

  • Calls that got one try

    Someone called back once, got voicemail, and the lead went cold.

  • Leads that piled up

    Forms, messages, and callbacks that stacked up after hours, on weekends, or during a slammed week.

  • "Call me later" people

    They were interested, just not that day, and nobody circled back.

  • Quotes that went quiet

    Estimates that never got a yes or a no because nobody followed up a second or third time.

How it works

No new software. Your team keeps doing what it does best.

  1. You send the leads that haven't booked

    A simple list or an export from whatever you already use.

  2. We agree on your rules

    A short call about who to book, who not to book, and what to confirm first. You review and sign them.

  3. We call, qualify, and book

    Real people follow up by phone and book the ones who fit your rules onto your calendar.

  4. You get every outcome

    Booked, not a fit, no longer needs it: everything is logged and reported back to you.
